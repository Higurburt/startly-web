Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026 · Effective: April 18, 2026

Startly is built for young entrepreneurs ages 8–19. This policy describes what we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights — including special protections for users under 13 (COPPA) and under 16 (GDPR-K).

1. Who we are

Startly is an app that helps young people go from a business idea to their first sale. Contact: support@startly.app.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: name, email, hashed password, age band (Under 13, 13–17, 18+).
  • Parent/guardian email: required for under-13, optional for 13–17.
  • Business data: business name, products, prices, sales, expenses, notes you type in.
  • Mentor chat: messages you send to the AI mentor.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous events like "task opened" or "sale logged".
  • Error reports: crash logs with Authorization headers stripped.
  • Technical data: browser, device type, IP address (security/fraud only).

3. How we use it

Run the app, give AI mentorship, track progress, secure the account, send transactional email, and study anonymous usage. We do not sell data, show ads, or train third-party AI on user data.

4. Children under 13 (COPPA)

A parent or guardian must confirm consent by clicking a link in an email we send them before any under-13 account is activated. Until then, no analytics are attached to the child's user id. Parents may review, export, or delete at any time from Account Settings or by emailing support@startly.app.

5. Children under 16 in the EU/UK (GDPR-K)

We apply the same parental consent flow to EU/UK users under 16.

6. Third-party processors

  • Railway (backend + Postgres, U.S.)
  • GitHub Pages (web hosting)
  • PostHog (anonymous product analytics)
  • Sentry (error tracking, PII stripped)
  • OpenAI / Anthropic (AI mentor responses)
  • SendGrid (transactional email)
  • Stripe (billing)
  • Google (optional OAuth sign-in)

7. Retention

  • Active accounts: kept while the account is active.
  • Inactive 24+ months: notified, then deleted.
  • Deleted accounts: soft-deleted 30 days, then permanent.
  • Encrypted backups: 30 days.
  • Billing records: 7 years (financial law).

8. Your rights

Access, export, correct, delete, opt out of analytics, object/restrict (EU/UK), and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Requests: support@startly.app.

9. Security

bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS/TLS 1.2+, encryption at rest, limited production access. We'll notify within 72 hours of a confirmed breach affecting your data.

10. International transfers

Servers are in the U.S. EU users: we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses with our processors.

11. Changes

Material changes are emailed 30 days before taking effect. See the "Last updated" date at the top for the current version.

12. Contact

support@startly.app — 14-day reply target, 30 days max under GDPR.
