Help & FAQ

Quick answers to the 10 things most people ask. Can't find what you need? Email support@startly.app.

Is Startly safe for kids?

Yes. Mentor responses are filtered for age-appropriate language. Under-13 accounts require a parent's verified email consent (COPPA) before activation. Parents can review, export, or delete data any time.

What data do you collect?

Name, email, hashed password, age band, business data you type in, anonymous usage events. No data sale, no ads, no training third-party AI on user data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel. Effective at end of the current period. First-time subscribers get a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Is the AI advice accurate?

Suggestions, not facts. Verify important decisions with a trusted adult or professional. Educational tool — not legal or financial advice.

Do parents have to approve everything?

Under 13: parent email required + consent link. 13–17: parent email collected, required before paid trial. 18+: no parent step.

How do refunds work?

First-time subscribers: 30-day money-back guarantee. Email support@startly.app. Renewals are non-refundable unless we cancel for cause.

What's the minimum age?

8. Users 8–12 need a parent's verified consent (COPPA). 13–17 need a parent's email before a paid trial.

I forgot my password. How do I get back in?

Login → Forgot password. You'll get a reset email within a minute. No access to the email? Contact support@startly.app from the signup email.

Is there a limit on AI mentor chats?

Free: 5/day. Premium: 100/day. Hit the cap? We show a friendly message and unlock the next day.

How do I contact a real person?

Email support@startly.app. 14-day reply target. Real human, not a bot.
